How to report your skips and targets
  1. Open the MSC reporting form and select your name.
  2. Choose Set or update my targets. Select a week and enter both your daily and weekly targets. Repeat for each previous week you have reported and for the current week.
  3. Choose Submit daily skips to record your skips or a rest day. To catch up, choose Submit a previous week's records and select that week.
  4. To change a target, submit new targets for the same week. To fix a skip count, choose Correct a previous entry.

Your dashboard updates automatically. If a day is unreported, leave it blank.
