How the numbers work
All displayed results come from participant submissions through the MSC Google Form. Identical repeat entries count once; valid corrections replace earlier values automatically. Ranking compares recorded skips with each participant's own weekly goals; unreported days are not treated as zero. The club goal totals submitted weekly targets and remains provisional while some targets are missing. Charts use accepted participant submissions; dates with no reports are never presented as confirmed zero-skip days. Member participation badges are celebratory, not proof of completion. Historical WhatsApp figures are excluded.
